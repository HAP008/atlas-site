# CONTENT RULES — voice and claims

## Audience
Regular, non-technical Mac users who want a Jarvis-like assistant that's simple and calm. Not developers. No jargon on public pages (no "MCP", "orchestrator", "tool use", "n8n", "SQLite", "tiers" as an internal term).

## Voice
- Plain, direct, sentence case. Active verbs. Specific over clever.
- Name things by what people do: "Connect Gmail", not "configure OAuth".
- A button says what happens: "Join the waitlist". The confirmation uses the same words: "You're on the waitlist."
- Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it. No apologies, no vagueness.
- Empty or not-connected states tell the visitor what to do next.
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "supercharge"). No "AI-powered" as a headline.

## What we may claim
Only things true on the day of writing and listed as shipped in `PROGRESS.md` or confirmed by Huzaifa. Everything else is "coming" with approved wording, or left out.

## What we never claim or fake
Testimonials, user counts, star ratings, customer logos, press mentions, benchmarks, uptime numbers, security certifications, "bank-level" or "military-grade" anything, compliance badges, that ATLAS "never makes mistakes", that it can't be copied.

## Required honesty lines (use the plain meaning, own wording)
- ATLAS runs on your Mac. We don't receive your data.
- Your requests go to the AI provider you connect, under their terms.
- ATLAS can make mistakes. It asks before it sends, writes or deletes.
- You can turn it off, export your data and delete it at any time.
- 18+ only.
- Pricing is a placeholder until confirmed.

## Legal pages
Plain English, short sentences. Open with "Draft: not yet reviewed by a lawyer or accountant." Use `TBC` for any missing business detail. Must match real behaviour; if the code doesn't do it, the page doesn't say it.

## Placeholders
Screenshot and video slots are labelled "screenshot coming" / "demo video coming". Placeholder prices are visibly marked. Never ship stock photos of fake users.
